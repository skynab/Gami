# Choosing the name Orikata

The app was called **Gami** until 27 November 2026, when it was renamed **Orikata**. This note records why,
what else was tried, and what's still open, so the decision doesn't have to be worked out again. It's a
screening by the owner (Zaro Software LLC) with Claude's help, not legal advice.

## Why Gami had to go

A US trademark search on 27 Nov 2026 found:

- **GÄMI** (Gami Technologies, Inc., serial 99406394) close to registration in classes 9 and 42 for mobile
  apps. The office ignores the umlaut, so it's the same word for the same kind of goods. An application for
  GAMI would very likely be refused, and using it risked a complaint to the app stores.
- **Gami Kids** and **PlayGAMI** already in the app stores.

Adding a word didn't help. **Gamifold** and **Gami 3D** both keep GAMI as the part people remember, and "3D"
only describes the app, so the office would set it aside and compare GAMI with GÄMI.

## Names tried

| Name | Result |
|---|---|
| Foldfinch, Foldkin, Tsurufold, Foldbloom, Kamifold | No conflicts found, but the owner didn't like them |
| Petalfold, Creaseworks, Foldtale | No conflicts found. Foldtale reads like a misspelt "folktale" |
| Valleyfold | A web agency, Valley Fold, uses valleyfold.app |
| Foldscape | Too close to Foldscope, which has a mobile game |
| Foldzoo | Too close to Foldify Zoo, a paper-craft app |
| Foldora | Already a Windows app |
| Paperwing | Too close to the game Paper Wings |
| Origamo | Registered in the US for paper goods |
| Wingfold | Pending in the US for financial services |
| Plica, Orizuru | Clear, but ordinary words ("fold" in Latin, "paper crane" in Japanese), so weak as brands |

Many marks use the word FOLD alone, including Fold, Inc.'s registration for apps (classes 9 and 42). Names that
pair "fold" with another word are usually fine, but a name without it avoids the question.

## Why Orikata

- **Its history.** *Orikata* (折形, "folded shapes") was the Japanese name for paper folding before "origami"
  took over in the late 1800s. The oldest known book of origami for fun, *Hiden Senbazuru Orikata* (1797),
  has it in its title.
- **The register.** No ORIKATA marks in the US. ORIKATO, ORIKASA and "ORI KATA" were also clear. ORIKAMI
  (Orikami Group B.V., a Dutch health-software firm) is the nearest, in a different field.
- **The stores and the web.** No app, game or product called Orikata.
- **Other countries.** In Italy, ORIKATA is registered by an event and conference company (orikata.com) in
  classes 35, 37, 41 and 42. That's a different field and covers only Italy. It matters only if the LLC files
  in Europe.

## The open question: ORIGATA

**ORIGATA** (Loxley Games, LLC, serial 97802246) is pending in the US for computer game software and online
games (classes 9 and 41). It's a filing on intent to use. Allowance came on 19 March 2024, and the fifth and
last extension was granted on 22 September 2026, so **the statement of use is due by 19 March 2027**. If Loxley
doesn't show a real game by then, the application lapses. No Origata game could be found anywhere.

The two views, both recorded:

- **The owner's:** in the US, Origata and Orikata are clearly different words, even though in Japanese they're
  the same 折形. Few American buyers know either word, so they won't treat them as one, and the products
  differ: one is a game, the other teaches origami. The risk seems low.
- **The caution:** the office compares sight, sound and meaning. The names differ by one letter and sound
  alike (o-ri-GA-ta, o-ri-KA-ta). The office also counts game software and apps as related goods, and it
  could treat them as the same Japanese word. If ORIGATA is still pending when an ORIKATA application is
  examined, a refusal or a wait is plausible.

Both agree that the risk mostly goes away if ORIGATA lapses in March 2027.

## Decisions and next steps

- The app uses **Orikata™** without a registration. In the US, rights come from use. Keep dated proof of first
  use: the launch date, store listing screenshots, the first sale and website snapshots.
- **After 19 March 2027, check [the ORIGATA record](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=97802246&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch).**
  If it has lapsed, file ORIKATA in class 9, about $350. If it has registered, talk to a trademark attorney
  before the brand grows.
- A short call with a trademark attorney before launch is still worth it.
- Domains: orikata.com, .app, .net, .org and getorikata.com are taken. orikata.io, orikata.co, orikata.studio,
  orikataapp.com, orikata3d.com, playorikata.com and tryorikata.com looked free on 27 Nov 2026 (DNS check
  only).

## What still says "gami"

These stay on purpose. Renaming them would lose people's saved work or break files already shared.

- The `gami:` fields in .fold files.
- The `gami.` keys in localStorage and the `gami` IndexedDB database.
- The repository's name and its GitHub Pages path, `/Gami/`.
- The applied Supabase migration, which is history.
